Ethel Cain - Willoughby Tucker, I'll Always Love You


This album manages to improve on the sound she engineered in her 2022 album 'preacher's daughter' while serving as its sequel. Some songs really stand out as imaginitive and as a continuation of her sound, but these tracks are unfortunately drowned out by a sea of singer-songwriter post-rock, and hardly groundbreaking lyrics.




6.5
