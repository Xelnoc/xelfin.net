Project Overload - New Beginnings


this is the 2024 debut from a small coventry band which has amassed thousands of spotify streams from only one album, promising a second in 2025. While nothing groundbreaking, they play with skill and effectively create an atmosphere of fun, bright indie pop which would please fans of the genre. Their lyrics are good but don't have much depth. Songs risk blending together, not doing enough on their own to stand out, and the album overall doesnt feel very complex. It succeeds in its goals and is a good all-round record.




6.5
